Search the director disqualifications register

This page lists recent director disqualification results obtained by the Insolvency Service or notified to us.

Each result includes:

  • the name of the person who was disqualified
  • the date the disqualification started
  • how long the disqualification lasts
  • details of the misconduct

This page may not include every recent disqualification result and should not be used as a complete record.

Supporting evidence

To complete this form, you need to attach evidence about your complaint at the end.

We can only look at your complaint using the information and evidence you give us.

In most cases, the authorising body can only uphold a complaint if there is evidence showing that the IP has done something wrong.

For a disciplinary investigation to go ahead, there needs to be clear evidence of wrongdoing by the IP, or by someone working under the IP’s direction.

What evidence should you upload?

Please upload anything you think supports your complaint, for example:

  • agreements between you and the IP (such as an IVA or settlement agreement)
  • emails, letters, or messages
  • a copy of the complaint you sent to the IP
  • the IP’s reply to your complaint
  • if your complaint is about an IVA, a copy of the IVA proposal and the chairman’s report
